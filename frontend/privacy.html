Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-05

What we collect

Only what verification needs: telegram_user_id, chat_id, optional username/first_name. No passwords, no session files. Verification tokens are stored as SHA-256 hashes.

How we use it

To complete verification, store connection for future notifications you approve, and log verification status per project. Queries are always scoped by project_id.

Retention

Verification sessions expire in 5 minutes. Connections remain until you delete the project or disconnect. Contact the project owner to remove your connection.

Contact

For data requests, contact the harry project owner via your integrating application.
